stat / display1,240 XP
The eyebrow is the system's signature move: mono, 11px, 600, 0.08em, uppercase, --muted. It does most of the work of making the app feel like an instrument. Use it; don't restyle it per screen.

Radius & elevation

9 / 11 / 16, then --sh-2. The odd radii are deliberate. Shadows are warm-tinted, never pure black.

The branch palette is cool and saturated — Tailwind-family hues that predate the current warm theme and sit apart from it. Colours are assigned by hashing the branch name, so a branch's colour is arbitrary and differs between projects. Several fail 3:1 against --bg at dot size. Open design question.
